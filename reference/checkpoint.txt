AMEEGO — Committed Final Product Direction
1. Final Core Concept
Ameego is an AI-powered formal speaking coach that helps users improve the soft skill of public communication in formal and public environments.
It is not limited to one specific situation like interviews or classroom presentations. Instead, Ameego focuses on improving the user’s overall ability to speak clearly, confidently, and effectively in formal/public settings.
It supports situations such as:
presentations
speeches
interviews
meetings
reports
academic defenses
leadership speaking tasks
The main goal is to help users improve how they speak, not just what they say.

2. Final Problem Statement
Many people have good ideas but struggle to express them clearly in formal or public speaking situations. Common problems include unclear articulation, too many filler words, poor pacing, weak structure, repetitive phrasing, and nervous delivery habits.
Traditional public speaking coaching is often expensive, time-consuming, or inaccessible, especially for students, job seekers, and young professionals.
Ameego solves this by giving users an accessible AI coach that analyzes their speech, gives measurable feedback, and guides them through a modular learning path based on their current skill level and weaknesses.

3. Final Target Users
Ameego is for people who want to improve formal/public speaking as a soft skill, including:
Students preparing for class presentations, reports, or thesis defenses
Job seekers preparing for interviews
Professionals preparing for meetings, proposals, and presentations
Team leaders, managers, and organization officers delivering speeches
Anyone who wants to improve their speaking ability in formal/public environments

4. Heavy MVP / Almost-Built Project Scope
Since your MVP is meant to be an almost already built product, the heavier feature set is acceptable. However, the features still need to be organized properly so judges see that the scope is intentional, not random.
MVP Feature Set
4.1 Pre-Test Assessment
Ameego begins with a speaking pre-test to evaluate the user’s current level.
The user may be asked to complete a short speaking task, such as:
introduce yourself formally
explain a simple topic
give a short opinion
deliver a one-minute presentation
The system then places the user into a skill level such as:
Beginner
Developing
Intermediate
Advanced
This pre-test helps Ameego personalize the user’s learning path.

4.2 Speech-to-Text Analysis
Ameego converts the user’s spoken input into text so the system can analyze the content of the speech.
It checks:
filler words
repeated words
sentence flow
vocabulary usage
clarity of ideas
organization of thoughts
This allows the app to identify the user’s speaking habits and weaknesses.

4.3 Filler Word Detection
Ameego detects common filler words such as:
um
uh
like
actually
basically
you know
so
The system shows how often these words appear and explains how they affect the speech.
Instead of only saying “you used too many filler words,” Ameego should give specific feedback like:
You used “um” 8 times and “like” 5 times. Most of these appeared before starting a new point. Try replacing fillers with a short pause.

4.4 Pacing Analysis
Ameego measures how fast or slow the user speaks.
It checks:
words per minute
long pauses
rushed sections
uneven pacing
The app then tells the user if their pacing is too fast, too slow, or appropriate for formal speaking.
This is important because speaking too fast can make the user sound nervous, while speaking too slowly can make the delivery feel weak or unprepared.

4.5 Speech Clarity Feedback
For the MVP, speech clarity should focus mainly on message clarity, not full pronunciation accuracy.
Ameego analyzes:
whether the message is understandable
whether the sentences are too long or confusing
whether the speaker repeats ideas too much
whether the main point is clear
Avoid claiming that the MVP can perfectly judge accent, pronunciation, or confidence. That can be improved later.

4.6 Speech Structure Feedback
Ameego checks if the user’s speech has a clear structure.
It evaluates whether the speech has:
an opening
main points
transitions
conclusion
clear flow of ideas
For example, if the user gives a formal answer, Ameego can check if the answer follows a clear structure such as:
Point → Explanation → Example → Closing
This makes the feedback more useful than just grammar correction.

4.7 Speaking Readiness Score
Ameego gives a Speaking Readiness Score based on measurable performance factors.
The score should be based on:
filler word frequency
pacing
clarity
structure
speech length
repetition
completion of the task
Do not call it a “confidence score” because true confidence is difficult to measure accurately. “Speaking Readiness Score” sounds more realistic and defensible.

4.8 Modular Learning Path
Ameego uses a modular learning path inspired by apps like Duolingo, but focused on formal/public speaking.
After the pre-test, the user is placed into a learning path based on their weaknesses.
Example modules:
Filler Word Control
Pacing and Pauses
Clear Sentence Delivery
Speech Structure
Storytelling
Persuasive Speaking
Formal Presentation Delivery
Impromptu Speaking
Q&A Response Practice
Final Speaking Challenge
Each module contains short drills that target a specific speaking skill.

4.9 AI-Driven Practice Drills
Ameego gives practice drills based on the user’s performance.
Examples:
Weakness Detected
Recommended Drill
Too many filler words
Pause Control Drill
Speaking too fast
Pacing Drill
Weak structure
Point-Example-Closing Drill
Unclear ideas
One-Minute Explanation Drill
Repetitive wording
Vocabulary Variety Drill
Poor closing
Strong Conclusion Drill

This makes the product feel personalized and useful.

4.10 Progress Dashboard
Ameego includes a dashboard that tracks user improvement over time.
The dashboard can show:
latest Speaking Readiness Score
filler word count
pacing score
clarity score
structure score
completed modules
practice streak
improvement history
Since this is a heavy MVP, the dashboard is acceptable, but it should stay clean and easy to understand.

4.11 AI Follow-Up Question Generator
This is a strong feature and more realistic than a full video call chatbot.
After the user finishes speaking, Ameego can ask follow-up questions such as:
Can you explain your second point more clearly?
Can you give a stronger example?
Can you summarize your main idea in one sentence?
How would you answer if the audience disagreed?
This helps users practice thinking and speaking under pressure.
This feature should be included if possible because it directly supports formal/public speaking improvement.

4.12 AMEEGO Buddy
The Ameego buddy can be included as a supporting UX/branding feature, not as the main product.
Its role should be:
guiding users through modules
explaining feedback in a friendly way
giving encouragement
making the app feel less intimidating
helping users stay consistent
This is good for branding, especially in a hackathon. But do not oversell it. The AI coach and feedback system are still the core.

5. Future Features
These are good features, but they should be placed under future development, not core MVP.
5.1 Camera-Based Body Language Analysis
This can analyze:
posture
eye contact
hand gestures
facial expressions
distracting mannerisms
Judge warning: This is useful, but it is technically heavy and privacy-sensitive. Only include it if your team can explain how you will protect user data.
Best wording:
Future body language analysis may use camera input to detect posture, eye contact, gestures, and distracting mannerisms to help users improve physical delivery.

5.2 AI Video Call Practice
This is also a good future feature, but it is too complex to be a required MVP feature.
It can be used for:
mock interviews
presentation Q&A
debate practice
meeting simulations
client pitch practice
Better future wording:
Ameego may later include AI-powered speaking simulations where users can practice with an AI audience, interviewer, or panel.

5.3 Voice Tone and Emotion Analysis
This can be useful, but it should be handled carefully.
Instead of saying the app detects emotions, say:
Ameego may analyze vocal delivery patterns such as tone variation, energy, volume, and monotone delivery.
This is safer and more realistic than claiming it can accurately detect emotions.

7. Final Unique Value Proposition
Ameego helps users improve formal and public speaking as a soft skill through AI-powered speech analysis, measurable feedback, and personalized modular training.
Unlike generic grammar or speech tools, Ameego focuses on the full speaking performance: pacing, filler words, clarity, structure, delivery habits, and continuous improvement.

8. Final Simple Pitch
Ameego is an AI-powered formal speaking coach that helps users improve public communication in formal and public environments. It starts with a pre-test, identifies the user’s speaking level, analyzes speech through AI, gives a Speaking Readiness Score, and recommends modular practice drills based on the user’s weaknesses. Through measurable feedback and guided practice, Ameego helps users become clearer, more confident, and more effective speakers.

9. Final Judge-Ready Verdict
Your product is now much stronger because it has a clearer direction: formal/public speaking improvement as a soft skill.
Since your MVP is supposed to be heavy and almost built, the feature set is acceptable, but only if you clearly separate:
Core MVP: speech analysis, scoring, modules, drills, dashboard
 Add-ons: AI follow-up questions, Ameego buddy
 Future features: camera analysis, video AI chatbot, tone/emotion analysis
The most important fix is to avoid sounding like you are building every possible communication feature. Present Ameego as a structured speaking improvement system, not just a random AI feedback app.
